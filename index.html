        Before you report

        Not all ditches are recorded with La Plata County. You can report damage even if the ditch or its name is missing from the map.

        1. Search for a street address or ditch name, or zoom to the location. You can expand the map for more room.
        2. Mark a single spot, or trace a damaged stretch from start to end.
        3. Follow the steps to describe the damage and give your contact information. Save each location, then send all your reports together.
