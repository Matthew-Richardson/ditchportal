        Before you report

        What to report

        Tell La Plata County about damage to ditches and irrigation infrastructure from the November 1 flood.

        How to report

        1. Find the damage by searching for an address or zooming in on the map.
        2. Mark one damaged spot or trace a damaged stretch.
        3. Describe the damage and enter your contact information. You can send several locations together.

        Some ditches are missing from the county map. You can still report damage there.

        How we use your report

        Reports help us assess damage and identify potential local, state, and federal assistance.

        Submitting a report does not guarantee assistance.
